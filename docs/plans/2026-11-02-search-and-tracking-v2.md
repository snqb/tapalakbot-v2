## Shared interfaces

- A durable per-conversation buying request lives in the existing clj-harness persisted session data, not in regex intent state.
- `tapalakbot.tracking/manage! [owner action spec]`: owner is server-supplied `{:id conversation-id :chat-id chat-id :thread-id thread-id}`; actions are `:create :list :update :pause :resume :stop`. The model cannot supply owner or delivery target. Specs use `:title :queries :price-min :price-max :city-id :notify-interval :excluded-ids :seed-ids :id` as applicable.
- Tracking results are maps with `:status` and `:track`/`:tracks` or safe `:error`; persisted rows retain snake_case fields. New tracking rows persist `chat_id`, `thread_id`, and EDN `excluded_ids`; `user_id` is the owning conversation ID. Legacy `tg-N` records retain their previous private delivery semantics and data.
- Tracker keeps full source/JEV semantics, treats excluded IDs as explicit user feedback, routes new rows by persisted chat/topic, and marks positive items seen only after confirmed delivery.
